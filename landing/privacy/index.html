Verbemo

Privacy

Verbemo has no account and no hosted meeting library. Recordings and the files made from them live in the recordings folder on your computer.

Meeting content

  • Recording, Parakeet, Whisper and GigaAM transcription, and the optional live transcript run locally. Ollama summaries stay on your computer when its Base URL is localhost/loopback; another host receives the summary input.
  • AssemblyAI, OpenAI or ElevenLabs transcription uploads meeting audio to the provider you choose.
  • Claude Code, Codex, Anthropic, and OpenAI summary or speaker-naming backends receive the transcript and available meeting context: title, call application, and, on macOS, calendar participants. Those tools may send it to their provider under your account’s terms.
  • API keys stay on your computer and are sent only to the provider they authenticate.

Calendar access is optional on macOS; Windows has no calendar integration. Calendar context stays local unless it is included in a request to a cloud or CLI model as described above.

Analytics disabled

The app and site do not collect usage statistics. There is no installation identifier, event queue or analytics server. Cloud meeting processors you select receive the data needed for that processing.
