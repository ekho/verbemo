## Platform

adaptive

## Product Purpose

Verbemo records meetings on the user's computer, turns recordings into transcripts and summaries, and makes the results available in a native meeting library.

## Operating Context

The app runs on macOS and Windows. Users can record from the microphone and call audio without a meeting bot. The meeting library supports reviewing earlier meetings while background work continues. Calendar context is available on macOS.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Meetings and their artifacts live in local folders. Processing can run locally or send meeting data to the selected cloud service or subscription CLI.
- The macOS Journal tracks background operations by meeting and distinguishes a cancellation request from its outcome. Existing audio and completed results are protected during cancellation.
- The desktop interface supports English and Russian. The activity bar keeps recording and processing status available in compact windows.
